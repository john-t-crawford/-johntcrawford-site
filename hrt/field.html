Photographs you take yourself are the only source with no licence obligation at all. Shoot the character, not the plant — a handsome portrait of a whole tree resolves nothing.

  1. Set your location in step 1. This decides which plants are proposed to you. It takes one tap.
  2. Read the goal in step 2. Three to five plants, chosen for where you are and what month it is.
  3. Tap a plant in the goal to load it into the camera form. Never type the species by hand if a goal or target row offers it.
  4. Take the photograph in step 3. The species and view are already filled in.
  5. Export in step 5 before you finish for the day. Shots live in this browser only until you do.

Storage is a convenience, not a backup Shots are held in this browser's database on this device. Clearing site data, private browsing, or a phone running low on space will drop them. Export at the end of every walk.

The export is a ZIP containing <id>-<view>.jpg files and a photos-manifest.json fragment crediting you. Unzip into ./photos/, merge the fragment, then run verify-photos.js and build-photos.js.
