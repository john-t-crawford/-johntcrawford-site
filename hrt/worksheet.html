Photograph sourcing worksheet

Thirty images, not one hundred and twenty-one. The drawn plates already carry every character the specs record; a photograph only earns its place where line work genuinely can't separate two species. Those places are listed below.

Each row names the view to source and the character the photograph must show. A pretty picture of the whole tree is worthless here — if the frame doesn't resolve the character, don't file it.

File naming for build-photos.js: <id>-<view>.jpg, views foliage | bark | habit | flower | fruit.

Cleanest source of all

Your own photographs. Maryland has every species on this list within an hour's drive. Shoot with a scale reference.

Run node verify-photos.js before node build-photos.js. It fails the build on a blocked licence, a missing credit, or a dead URL.

Tier A — the twelve clusters (24 images)

These are the pairs where learners actually lose points.

Confusable clusters
#ClusterViewThe character the photo must show
1Quercus rubra vs Q. palustrisfoliageSinus depth. Red oak sinuses cut roughly halfway to the midrib; pin oak's cut nearly to it, leaving narrow struts. Frame both blades flat.
2Carpinus caroliniana vs Ostrya virginianabarkThe decisive character, and drawing can't do it justice — smooth sinewy fluting versus fine shreddy strips. Trunk at close range.
3Taxodium distichum vs Metasequoia glyptostroboidesfoliageNeedle arrangement on the twig: alternate versus strictly opposite. Macro, twig filling the frame.
4Ilex crenata vs I. glabra vs Buxus sempervirensfoliageThree images. Crenate versus entire margins, alternate versus opposite arrangement. Twig tips, not hedge faces.
5Cornus florida vs C. kousabarkAlligator-hide blocks versus mottled exfoliating patches.
6Ulmus americana vs Zelkova serratafoliageLeaf base symmetry — strongly oblique in elm, near-even in zelkova.
7Viburnum dentatum vs Aronia arbutifoliafoliageOpposite versus alternate. One image of each showing a node.
8Pinus virginiana vs P. nigrafoliageNeedle length and twist in a two-needle fascicle. Include a ruler.
9Picea abies vs Abies concolorfoliageNeedle cross-section and attachment: four-sided on a peg versus flat and stalkless.

Verification

Before a file goes in ./photos/, confirm the species against a herbarium or flora record — the Maryland Plant Atlas or the USDA PLANTS profile, not a gardening blog. A misidentified photograph in an identification trainer teaches the error, and the learner has no way to catch it. This is the one step that cannot be automated, which is why the manifest leaves src blank rather than guessing.
