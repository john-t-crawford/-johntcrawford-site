What these are

A drawn plate isolates a character. A photograph shows the plant as you will actually meet it. Neither is sufficient alone, which is why these sheets put them side by side — the generated plate above, your own photographs below, at whichever scales you captured. Call it a plant's ID photo paired with its yearbook picture: one for the facts, one for the personality.

Do this Use the filter to jump to a species, or scroll. Each photograph names its view and carries its credit. Tap any photograph to open it full size.
