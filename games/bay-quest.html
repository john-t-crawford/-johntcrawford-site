Blue Crab Biology

Callinectes sapidus: The "Beautiful Savory Swimmer". Nano AI detects unique growth plate patterns in blue crabs. Did you know they molt their entire shell to grow?

"Nano Crab Note: Male crabs have a T-shaped abdomen (the 'Washington Monument'), while females have a rounded one (the 'Capitol Dome')."

About This Project

Bay Quest is a gamified environmental-education piece built to make Chesapeake Bay ecology engaging for a broad adult audience. It uses Maryland DNR reference data on blue crab biology and invasive species to turn static facts into an active, scored challenge — then layers an on-device "Nano Crab" AI guide on top to surface facts contextually as you play.

Part of the Interactive Learning series at johntcrawford.com/games.
