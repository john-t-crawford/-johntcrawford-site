Executive Summary

WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, introduces nine new or revised success criteria with direct impact on federal e-learning, training portals, and digital job aids. For government contractors and federal agencies, the update represents both a compliance obligation and a market differentiator.


Most contractors treat WCAG compliance as a cost center. The instructional designers who understand it as a capability differentiator win more work and command higher rates. If you build to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, you automatically satisfy WCAG 2.1 and WCAG 2.0 requirements as well — making WCAG 2.2 the single safest target for any government-facing training deliverable.

WCAG 2.2 QA Checklist — Sample

The full 17-point checklist is in the PDF download. Here's a preview:

  • All keyboard-navigable elements have a visible focus indicator ≥2px wide with 3:1 contrast AA
  • All dragging interactions have a single-pointer (click) alternative AA
  • All interactive targets meet minimum 24×24px size AA
  • Help mechanism (if present) appears in consistent location across all pages A
  • Authentication does not require solving a cognitive puzzle without an alternative AA
  • All images have meaningful alt text (or empty alt for decorative) A
  • All video content has synchronized captions and audio description AA
