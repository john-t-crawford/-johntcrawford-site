Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

This page describes what happens when you submit a form on johntcrawford.com. There is no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party data sharing anywhere on this site.

What's collected

If you submit a download or contact form, the following is recorded:

  • The email address and name you provide
  • Which form you submitted (so I know what you were asking about)
  • Your IP address and browser user-agent string, used only for spam/abuse prevention

This is stored in a private log file on the server, outside the public web directory, and is not accessible from the internet.

What it's used for

  • Sending you the resource you requested (e.g., a download link)
  • Notifying me that someone made a request, so I can follow up if appropriate
  • Rate-limiting submissions from the same IP address to block automated spam

What it's not used for

  • No mailing list, newsletter, or automated marketing sequence
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of your information with any third party
  • No advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies on this site

How long it's kept

Submission records are kept indefinitely in the private log for my own recordkeeping, unless you ask me to delete yours — see below.

Your choices

Email contact@crawfordcreationsllc.com at any time to ask what information I have on file, or to have it deleted. I'll respond directly, no automated system involved.

Questions

This is a one-person site — if anything here is unclear, just ask: contact@crawfordcreationsllc.com.
