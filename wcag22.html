Why this guide exists

Most accessibility guidance lives in two places: the W3C's normative spec (rigorous but dense) and vendor blog posts (digestible but incomplete). Federal contractors need a third option — a practical reference that holds up under a Trusted Tester evaluation but reads like it was written by someone who has shipped a deliverable.
